I spend a lot of time moving between things that don't really fit neatly into one category so I wanted to create somewhere to keep the things I build and the ideas I want to remember  without turning them into LinkedIn posts or pretending they're finished.

So this site is deliberately small. Projects when there's something to show. Notes when there's something worth writing down. Everything else can stay off the internet.
